# Glossary

**Project**: A development or property shown on the website. Managed from its own **Projects** item in the sidebar, not as a page.

**Inquiries / Team & Reviews**: Sidebar groups. *Inquiries* holds project and general inquiries; *Team & Reviews* holds employees and testimonials.

**Featured**: A flag that controls whether a testimonial or staff member appears on the public website.

**Published**: For projects, whether it appears on the website at all. For pages, whether your edits are live.

**Status**: A project's lifecycle stage, which determines whether it falls under the *Active* or *Past* filter.

**Service**: A category of work with its own page under Services, used to group and filter projects.
