# Branding & settings

Go to **Settings → Branding & Site Settings**. Everything here applies across the whole site. The page is split into four tabs; click a tab name at the top to switch:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Branding** | Site name, logo, colours |
| **Contact & social** | Phone, email, maps, social links |
| **Site** | Default currency, breadcrumb style |
| **Projects** | Project page options, land plan services |

Press **Save** once when you're done. It saves every tab together, so you can change things on several tabs first.

## Identity {#identity}

*Branding tab.*

- Site name: shown in the browser title and the footer
- Logo: the navbar image
- Primary and secondary colours: entered as hex values

## Contact and social {#contact-social}

*Contact & social tab.*

- Public phone number and email address
- Links for Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok. Leave any blank to hide that icon.

## Maps {#maps}

*Contact & social tab.*

- **Maps URL**: the Google Maps share link behind the *Get Directions* button
- **Maps embed URL**: the embedded map. Get it from Google Maps → Share → Embed a map, and copy the `src` value out of the iframe.

## Currency {#currency}

*Site tab.*

Default currency for project prices site-wide. Manage the available currencies under **Settings → Currencies**, where each has a code, name and symbol.

## Project page layout {#project-layout}

*Projects tab, except the breadcrumb style, which is on the Site tab.*

- Show or hide the key stats strip
- Keep the inquiry button visible while scrolling
- Show or hide the *More Projects* section, and set how many appear (up to 6)
- Wording of the message shown when a project is sold
- Breadcrumb style on interior pages

## Land plan annotations {#land-plan-services}

On the **Projects** tab, under **Land Plan Annotations (services)**, tick the services whose projects can have a boundary and pin drawn on a photo. Land Acquisition & Sales is ticked to begin with. Projects in any other service won't show the drawing tools. See [Mark a boundary and pin on a land photo](/cms/manual/projects/#land-plan).

## Email wording {#email-wording}

Under email settings you can change the subject line and opening line of the automatic confirmation emails for project inquiries and general inquiries. These include placeholders, `{project}` for the project name and `{name}` for the customer's name, which are filled in automatically. Keep the placeholders intact when editing.
