# Getting started

This manual shows you how to look after the website: reading messages from customers, adding properties, and keeping the content up to date. You don't need any technical knowledge.

## Where to log in {#sign-in}

The place where you manage the website is called the **CMS** (short for "content management system"). Think of it as the back office of the website.

**Open it here:** [[cms_url]]

1. Open the address above in your web browser.
2. Type in your staff username and password.
3. Tick the "I'm not a robot" box when it asks, then sign in.

You'll arrive on your **dashboard**, the first page you see each time you log in.

**Good to know:** This is a different login from the one customers use on the public website. A customer account will not get you in here. If you can't log in, ask an administrator to check that you have a staff account.

## Finding your way around {#where-things-are}

The menu down the left-hand side is how you move around. Here is what each part is for:

| If you want to… | Click… |
| --- | --- |
| See what's new since you last logged in | **Dashboard**, then the "New Unread" box at the top |
| Read a message from a customer | **Inquiries** |
| Show or hide a customer review on the website | **Team & Reviews → Testimonials** |
| Update your team's details | **Team & Reviews → Employees** |
| Add or change a property | **Projects** |
| Change the wording or pictures on a page | **Pages** |
| Change the logo, colours or contact details | **Settings → Branding & Site Settings** |
| Change the currency used for prices | **Settings → Currencies** |
| Change the wording of automatic emails to customers | **Settings → Email settings** |

Not sure where something is? The **Help** item in the left-hand menu brings you back to this manual at any time.
