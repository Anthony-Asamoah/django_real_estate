# Inquiries

## Handle project and general inquiries {#handle-inquiries}

**Goal:** Read and act on a customer inquiry.

1. In the CMS sidebar go to **Inquiries → Project Inquiries** or **General Inquiries**.
2. The list shows the sender's name, the project or service, contact details, and when it arrived.
3. Click an entry to read the full message.
4. Reply to the customer using their email address or phone number. **Replies are sent outside the CMS**, from your own email or phone.

**Important:** Inquiry records are **read-only**. They are a permanent log of what the customer actually submitted and cannot be edited from the CMS. The only change you can make is marking them read.
