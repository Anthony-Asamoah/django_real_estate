# Staff members

## Manage staff members {#manage-staff}

Go to **Team & Reviews → Employees**. Each staff member record holds a name, role, description, email, phone, photo and hire date, and can be marked as **featured** to appear in the team section of the website.

Staff members are also what the **Lead staff member** field on a project points at, and customers can filter the projects list by lead, so keeping these records current affects more than just the team page.
