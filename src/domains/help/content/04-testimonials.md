# Testimonials

## Approve and publish a testimonial {#publish-testimonial}

**Who:** Staff with permission to change testimonials.

1. Go to **Team & Reviews → Testimonials**.
2. Read the submission. Use the **Featured** and **Read** filters to find what you need.
3. Tick the checkbox next to one or more testimonials.
4. Choose **Show on website** from the bulk actions and confirm.

The selected testimonials now appear in the testimonials section of the website.

To take one down, select it and choose **Hide from website**.

**Why it works this way:** Testimonial records are read-only, so showing and hiding is done through these bulk actions rather than by editing the record. This keeps the customer's words exactly as they submitted them.
