# New submissions

## The dashboard notifications panel {#notifications-panel}

**Goal:** See what's come in since you last looked, and clear it.

1. On the CMS dashboard, look for the **New Unread** panel at the top. It only appears when something is waiting.
2. The panel breaks the count down into three lines: **Project Inquiries**, **General Inquiries** and **Testimonials**, each with an unread count.
3. Click a line to open that list in full.
4. Click **Mark all read** on a line to clear its counter without opening it.

Counts drop to zero and the panel disappears once everything is read.
